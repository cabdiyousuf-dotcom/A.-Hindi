About

A career built at the intersection of policy and people

I have spent more than twenty years leading complex, multi-stakeholder programs in fragile and conflict-affected environments, and in Canadian settlement and employment services. My work has always sat at the same intersection: turning policy into programs that actually reach people.

In Somalia, I served as Minister of Humanitarian Affairs & Disaster Management and, before that, Minister of Planning & International Cooperation for the Regional Government of Northeast Somalia (SSC-Khatumo), working with UN agencies, donors, and local communities on humanitarian coordination, disaster response, and development planning in one of the country's most complex operating environments.

In Toronto, I managed an Employment Services team delivering IRCC, Ontario Trillium Foundation, and City of Toronto-funded programs, securing more than $1 million in grant funding, and spent over a decade before that coordinating school-based settlement programs for newcomer families across the city.

I have a background in Public Policy and Canadian Public Administration from Toronto Metropolitan University. I'm fluent in Somali, Arabic, and English, and I stay closely connected to Toronto's newcomer-serving community as much as to the governance and humanitarian networks I worked within across the Horn of Africa.
