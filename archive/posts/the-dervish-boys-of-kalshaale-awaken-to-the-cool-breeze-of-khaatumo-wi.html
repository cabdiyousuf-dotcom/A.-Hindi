The Dervish boys of Kalshaale awaken to the cool breeze of Khaatumo winds throughout SSC and TFG

Published without a byline

March 8, 2012

On March 5, 2012, the Transitional Federal Government of Somalia has recognized Khaatumo State of Somalia as a full-fledged state and part and parcel of the of the federal government since it has fulfilled all the required conditions laid for federal states. This was a pleasing news for the people of Khaatumo state and their friends who stood beside them in their protracted struggle for statehood.

http://sscradio.com/playaudio.php?id=130

The people of Khaatumo State of Somalia are totally committed to Somali unity and territorial integrity of Somalia. The people of Khaatumo state of Somalia are dedicated to this patriotic cause and shall never deviate from this course. This honorable stand has earned them praise and acclaim on the part of true compatriots and fellow Somali countrymen.

The regime in Hargeisa is advised to re-connect with reality and withdraw its armed militia from all the territory that belongs to Khaatumo state as early as possible. That is the right thing to do. It is unwise to drag the two peoples to destructive internecine fighting. It must be understood that the people of Khaatumo state cannot be subjugated at gun point. The giant Darwiish is now awake, aware and alert.
Since its inception and inauguration on January 12, 2012, Khaatumo state of Somalia took large strides and made significant progress. There has been a big shift in the awareness of the people. The people of the region are now united and holding together firmly, and their morale is high. But the situation still delicate since many parts of Khaatumo’s territory, including the capital city of Las Anod, remains under occupation by northern Somali tribesmen militia.

Khaatumo state of Somalia is run by a pick out or a select team with firm resolve and broad vision. The team’s mission is to re-shape and re-build Khaatumo state from scratch. Khaatumo’s people and their ruling body are also ready and willing to play a big role in the redemption of Somalia. Khaatumo state is like a shining pole-star for Somalia..

Khaatumo state is ready to maintain a close bond and cooperation with its neighbors to the west and east. Khaatumo state is willing to patch up and mend relations with the neighbors on the basis of good neighborly relations, mutual respect and mutual assistance. But first and foremost, the neighbors must recognize the right of Khaatumo people to self-rule so that they could manage their own local affairs. The neighbors had better understand that cooperation is better than confrontation, and that peace is the best bet. The two regimes in Hargeisa and Growe must stop their deliberate and persistent sabotage and machinations against the nascent Khaatumo State of Somalia. You cannot succeed to undermine, wreck or ruin or try to bring Khaatumo state to a halt no matter how hard you try. This is because the people of Khaatumo state are vigilant and watchful.

Mohamed Ali Dad, San Diego, CA, USA
