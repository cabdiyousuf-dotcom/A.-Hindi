Kh-State missing at the Aid donor tables

By A. Hindi

February 16, 2012

Khaatumo Kids are waiting for you!

“Cidina uma maqna Ceelna um qodna” is phrase we often hear in our literature. Check out in the attached document [read document below] how the other Somali Regions are represented at the donor and Aid tables while the SSC regions are shut out; one might wonder why! Ever heard of the phrase that says ” 99 percent of Opportunities is simply “showing up” ? well it appears that we have been absent from the Aid donor tables or ineffective thus letting our people down by not refining our organizational structures both at the SSC and Khaatumo level.

The children of Khaatumo State have been waiting and they continue to wait patiently as they showed up in droves during the welcoming celebrations of the Khaatumo State with one simple dream; a wish that someday a TRUE AND REPRESENTATIVE administration and Regional Government will answer their call. Who do you think these kids should be waiting for! Our NEIGHBOURS have let them down; check out number of schools, hospitals and jobs they were invited to. not much my friends. I say to you my Kids; Wait no more. KHAATUMO  STATE OF SOMALIA  has arrived !!! ”We are the ones we have been waiting for”
