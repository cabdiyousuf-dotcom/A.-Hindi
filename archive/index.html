About this archive

khatumo.net went offline years ago. These pages were recovered from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine and are reproduced as they were originally published — the writing has not been edited, updated, or corrected, and reflects the politics of its moment rather than my present views.

It was a publication with contributors, not a personal blog. Every entry carries the byline it carried originally; where a piece ran without one, that is noted rather than filled in. Copyright in each piece rests with its author. If you wrote something here and would like it removed or corrected, get in touch.

Some images did not survive the archiving and have been omitted rather than shown broken.
